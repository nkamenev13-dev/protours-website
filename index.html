Good to know

Your questions,
answered.

Where will you pick me up?+

We collect you directly from your hotel or accommodation in Paris. Your exact pickup time is confirmed before the tour.

Is this a guided tour?+

Your day is hosted by a professional driver-companion who takes care of the journey and practical details. This is not a licensed guided tour inside the monuments.

How can I pay?+

After availability is confirmed, you can pay through a secure payment link or choose to pay on the day of your tour.

What vehicle do you use?+

We use a comfortable passenger car or minivan depending on the size of the group.

How many guests are needed for a tour?+

Our shared day trips operate from 3 guests. We confirm availability after receiving your request.

Does the driver speak English?+

Yes. Your English-speaking driver-host accompanies you on the road and helps with practical details. The driver is not a licensed guide inside monuments or attractions.

When will my booking be confirmed?+

We normally contact you within 1–2 hours to confirm availability, pickup details and payment options.

Are entrance tickets and meals included?+

Inclusions may vary by destination. We explain exactly what is included when confirming your selected tour.

Can I travel with children or luggage?+

Please tell us when we contact you. We will confirm the suitable vehicle and any practical arrangements for your group.

What happens in bad weather?+

Tours normally operate in changing weather conditions. If travel is not safe or a major attraction is unavailable, we will contact you to discuss the available options.
